Our Stall Design Process

From concept to completion in 4 simple steps.

1. Consultation

You provide your requirements (stall size, colors) and your company logo as a `.cdr` file.

2. Free 3D Design

We create a custom 3D model of your stall and send it to you on WhatsApp for your approval.

3. Precision Fabrication

Once approved, our team builds every component of your stall using high-quality materials.

4. On-Site Installation

We deliver and professionally install your stall at the exhibition, ensuring it's perfect for the event.
